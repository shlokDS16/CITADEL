# Government Identity & Civic Services

Citizen-facing guidance for core Government of India identity and civic
documents. All information here is public and may be shared with any
citizen.

## Aadhaar

### What Aadhaar is
Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by UIDAI to residents
of India, linked to biometric (fingerprints, iris, photo) and demographic
data. It is the most widely used proof of identity and address.

### How to enrol for a new Aadhaar
1. Locate the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or enrolment centre via the
   official UIDAI portal (uidai.gov.in) using your PIN code.
2. Carry one Proof of Identity (passport, voter ID, driving licence,
   PAN) and one Proof of Address (utility bill, bank passbook, rent
   agreement). A full list of accepted documents is on uidai.gov.in.
3. At the centre your demographic details are recorded and biometrics
   (ten fingerprints, both iris scans, photograph) are captured. This
   takes about 15 minutes.
4. You receive an acknowledgement slip with a 14-digit Enrolment ID
   (EID) and timestamp.
5. The Aadhaar letter is generally delivered by post within 60–90 days;
   you can download an e-Aadhaar earlier once generated.

### Tracking and downloading Aadhaar
Use the EID on the official UIDAI resident portal to check status and
download a password-protected e-Aadhaar PDF (password is first 4 letters
of name in capitals + birth year).

### Updating Aadhaar (address, mobile, name, biometrics)
- Address can be updated online through the official self-service update
  portal with a valid proof of address.
- Name, date of birth, gender and mobile number updates may require a
  visit to an enrolment centre.
- A nominal government fee applies for updates; the exact current fee is
  provided live by the CITADEL system and shown when you ask.
- UIDAI periodically asks residents to re-verify or update documents;
  follow only official UIDAI communication.

### Aadhaar safety
Never share your Aadhaar OTP. Use a Virtual ID (VID) or masked Aadhaar
where possible. Lock/unlock biometrics from the official portal.

## PAN Card

### What PAN is
Permanent Account Number is a 10-character alphanumeric ID issued by the
Income Tax Department, mandatory for tax filing, high-value transactions
and many financial services.

### Applying for PAN
1. Apply online through an authorised PAN service provider (Protean /
   UTIITSL) or the Income Tax e-filing portal.
2. Choose Form 49A (Indian citizens). e-KYC via Aadhaar OTP allows a
   fully paperless application.
3. Pay the processing fee online.
4. e-PAN is typically issued within a few working days; the physical
   card follows by post.

### Instant e-PAN
Eligible individuals with a valid Aadhaar linked to an active mobile
number can get a free instant e-PAN through the Income Tax e-filing
portal in minutes.

### PAN–Aadhaar linking
PAN must be linked with Aadhaar as per Income Tax rules. An unlinked PAN
may become inoperative. Link and check status on the Income Tax e-filing
portal; a late-linking fee may apply as notified.

## Passport

### Applying for a passport
1. Register on the Passport Seva portal and fill the application.
2. Pay the fee online and book an appointment at a Passport Seva Kendra
   (PSK) or Post Office PSK.
3. Carry original documents: proof of present address, proof of date of
   birth, and identity documents as listed for your category.
4. Police verification is usually done before or after issuance.
5. Track status online using the file number / ARN.

### Tatkaal passport
Tatkaal scheme offers faster processing for an additional fee, subject
to eligibility and document verification.

### Renewal / re-issue
Apply for re-issue when the passport is near expiry, exhausted of pages,
or details change. Carry the old passport.

## Voter ID (EPIC) & Electoral Services

- Register as a voter or apply for corrections through the official
  Election Commission voter services portal once you are 18+.
- You can apply for a new EPIC, correction, address shift, or download a
  digital voter ID (e-EPIC).
- Verify your name in the electoral roll before every election.

## Driving Licence & Vehicle Documents

### Learner's licence and driving licence
1. Apply on the official Parivahan Sarathi portal for a Learner's
   Licence; pass the online test on traffic rules.
2. After the mandatory waiting period, book a driving test for the
   permanent Driving Licence.
3. Carry age proof, address proof and medical certificate where
   required.

### Vehicle registration (RC), insurance, PUC
- New vehicles are registered (RC) through the dealer / RTO on Parivahan.
- Valid third-party insurance and a Pollution Under Control (PUC)
  certificate are legally mandatory to drive.
- RC transfer, address change, hypothecation removal and duplicate RC
  are available on the Parivahan portal.

## Certificates (Birth, Death, Income, Caste, Domicile)

- Birth and death certificates are issued by the local municipal body /
  registrar; apply within the prescribed days of the event for the
  simplest process; delayed registration needs additional steps.
- Income, caste, domicile and residence certificates are issued by the
  revenue department / SDM office, often available via the state
  e-district portal with supporting proofs.

## Property & Land Records

- Land records, mutation status and encumbrance certificates are
  available through state land-record portals (e.g., Bhulekh /
  Dharani-type systems depending on the state).
- Property registration and stamp duty payment are handled at the
  Sub-Registrar office; many states allow online appointment and
  e-stamping.

## DigiLocker

DigiLocker is the official government digital document wallet. Aadhaar,
driving licence, RC, education certificates and more can be fetched as
legally valid digital copies and shared securely. Citizens are
encouraged to use DigiLocker instead of carrying physical documents.

## General guidance

- Always use official government domains for applications and payments.
- Government departments will never ask for OTP or full card numbers
  over phone or chat.
- For exact current fees, deadlines and rule changes, ask this assistant
  — it reflects the latest values configured in the CITADEL system.
