# Taxes, Revenue & Business Registration

Public guidance for individual taxpayers and small businesses. Exact
rates, slabs and due dates change every financial year — ask the
assistant for current configured values and always confirm on the
official Income Tax / GST portals.

## Income Tax (Individuals)

### Filing an Income Tax Return (ITR)
1. Register/log in on the official Income Tax e-filing portal with PAN.
2. Pick the correct ITR form for your income type (salary, business,
   capital gains, etc.).
3. Pre-filled data (salary, TDS, interest) is largely auto-populated;
   verify against Form 26AS / AIS.
4. Pay any balance tax, submit, and e-verify (Aadhaar OTP, net-banking,
   etc.) within the allowed window — return is invalid if not verified.

### Refunds
Refunds are credited to the pre-validated bank account; track status on
the e-filing portal. Keep the bank account PAN-linked and validated.

### Form 26AS / AIS
Annual Information Statement and Form 26AS show your TDS, high-value
transactions and reported income — reconcile before filing.

## GST (Businesses)

- Register for GST on the official GST portal when turnover crosses the
  notified threshold or for inter-state / e-commerce supply.
- File the applicable periodic returns (e.g., GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) by their
  due dates; late filing attracts late fee and interest.
- Composition scheme offers simplified compliance for small eligible
  taxpayers.

## Property & Other Local Taxes

- Property tax is paid to the municipal corporation; most cities offer
  online payment by property ID with early-payment rebates.
- Professional tax (where applicable by state) is deducted by employers
  or paid by professionals.
- Road tax is paid at registration via Parivahan.

## Business Registration

### MSME / Udyam
Register free on the official Udyam Registration portal with Aadhaar and
PAN to get a Udyam certificate, enabling priority-sector benefits,
tender preferences and scheme access.

### Startup India
Eligible startups can get DPIIT recognition on the Startup India portal
for tax and compliance benefits, subject to conditions.

### Other registrations
Shops & Establishment (state labour portal), Import-Export Code (DGFT),
FSSAI (food businesses), and trade licences (municipal) as applicable to
the activity.

## Tips

- Keep PAN, Aadhaar and bank details linked and validated.
- Maintain digital copies of returns and receipts in DigiLocker.
- Beware of fake "refund" links — the department communicates only
  through the official portal/email; never share OTP or full card data.
