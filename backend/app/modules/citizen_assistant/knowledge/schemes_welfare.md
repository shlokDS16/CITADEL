# Welfare Schemes, Health, Pension & Education

Public guidance on major citizen welfare schemes. Eligibility and
benefit amounts are set by government and may change; ask the assistant
for the current configured values where applicable.

## Health

### Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
- Provides health cover for eligible families for secondary and tertiary
  hospitalisation at empanelled hospitals.
- Check eligibility on the official PM-JAY portal using mobile/ration
  details; generate the Ayushman card via the official app/portal.
- Cashless treatment is at empanelled hospitals; keep the Ayushman card
  and an ID.

### ABHA (Health ID)
ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) is a digital health ID to link and
share health records with consent. Create it on the official ABHA portal.

### Birth/Death & vaccination records
Birth/death certificates: local municipal registrar. Vaccination
certificates: official health portal (e.g., CoWIN-type systems).

## Agriculture & Rural

### PM-KISAN
Income support to eligible landholding farmer families in instalments to
the bank account. Register/verify status with Aadhaar on the official
PM-KISAN portal; complete e-KYC to keep instalments active.

### MGNREGA
Guarantees wage employment days per year to rural households. Apply for a
job card at the Gram Panchayat; wages are paid to the linked bank
account.

### Ration Card / NFSA
Subsidised foodgrain for eligible households via the Public Distribution
System. Apply/port through the state food & civil supplies portal; One
Nation One Ration Card allows access across states.

## Pension & Provident

### EPF (Employees' Provident Fund)
- Check balance/passbook via the EPFO member portal or UMANG using UAN.
- Withdrawal/advance and transfer on job change are done online with a
  KYC-seeded UAN.

### APY (Atal Pension Yojana)
Guaranteed pension after 60 for subscribers (typically unorganised
sector) based on contribution and joining age. Enrol via your bank.

### NPS (National Pension System)
Voluntary, market-linked retirement savings with tax benefits; open via
eNPS or a Point of Presence (bank).

## Education

### Scholarships (NSP)
The National Scholarship Portal hosts central/state scholarships
(pre-matric, post-matric, merit, minority, disability). Apply with
Aadhaar, bank and institution details within the notified window; keep
documents verified by the institution.

### DigiLocker for certificates
Board and university results/certificates can be fetched in DigiLocker
as legally valid copies for admissions and jobs.

### Student loans / Vidya Lakshmi
Education-loan applications to multiple banks via the Vidya Lakshmi
portal; interest subvention may apply for eligible categories.

## How to use this assistant for schemes

Ask things like "Am I eligible for Ayushman Bharat?", "How do I check
PM-KISAN status?", or upload a document (e.g., income certificate) and
ask which schemes you may qualify for. The assistant will guide you to
the official process. It will not ask for or store OTPs, full bank
numbers or passwords.
